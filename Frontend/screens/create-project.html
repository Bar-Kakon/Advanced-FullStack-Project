לוח ימי עבודה Working calendar

הפרויקט מתוזמן לפי ימי עבודה ולא לפי ימי לוח, וכל מי שמשתתף בו מתוזמן לפי הלוח של הפרויקט. The project is scheduled by working days rather than calendar days, and everyone taking part in it is scheduled by the project's calendar.

ל־FieldSync אין שבוע עבודה קבוע. הלוח שמופיע כאן הוא ברירת המחדל שהגדרתם לעצמכם — לקבלנים אחרים יש לוחות אחרים, ויש שעובדים בשישי ויש שלא. FieldSync has no fixed working week. The calendar shown here is the default you set for yourself — other contractors have different ones, and some work on Friday while others do not.

What happens to a deleted project — its tasks, its files, its messages, its history and its members — has not been defined in the product yet. The action appears here to fix its placement and its character, and it does nothing in the prototype.
